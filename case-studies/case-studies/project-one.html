Photo Essay Project (CMI 3310)

Overview

This project focused on creating a visual photo essay that tells a story through a series of curated images.

Challenge

The challenge was to communicate a clear narrative using only images while maintaining composition and consistency.

My Role

I planned, shot, and edited all images, then structured them into a cohesive visual sequence.

Final Work

Results

The final essay successfully communicated a visual narrative with strong composition and storytelling flow.

Reflection

This project improved my understanding of visual storytelling and composition techniques.
